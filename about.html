About

Marketing intelligence for serious authors.

I combine book-market research, reader intelligence and email marketing thinking to help authors make clearer, evidence-informed decisions.

The Philosophy

Understand first. Act with purpose.

Marketing gets stronger when assumptions are separated from evidence and attention is directed toward the opportunities that matter most.

The goal is not to make promises about sales, reviews or outcomes. It is to improve the quality of the information behind a marketing decision—and turn that information into practical action.

Working Principles

A disciplined approach to a noisy market.

Evidence-led

Use observable signals and credible sources before making strategic claims.

Reader-aware

Pay attention to the language, expectations and behavior readers actually display.

Priority-driven

Rank opportunities instead of producing a long list of equally weighted recommendations.
