Marketing starts by understanding the book.

Orynne is built around a simple principle: before deciding what to promote, where to promote it or how much to spend, understand the book, its readers and the path between them.

01 — Understand

Look before prescribing

We begin with the book, its positioning, the surrounding online presence and the questions the author is actually trying to solve.

02 — Prioritize

Separate signal from activity

Not every marketing task deserves equal attention. The aim is to identify the decisions with the clearest relevance.

03 — Direct

Turn understanding into action

Useful analysis should lead somewhere: clearer positioning, stronger discovery paths and more deliberate next steps.

The Orynne approach

Start with the situation. Then decide what deserves attention.

Orynne is not built around a fixed promotional sequence applied to every author. The process is intended to establish context before recommending activity.

1

Understand the concern

Define what is happening, what the author is trying to achieve and what remains uncertain.

2

Examine the surrounding signals

Review relevant discovery, positioning, visibility and reader-path factors rather than assuming the cause.

3

Identify the gaps

Separate what is already working from what may be unclear, disconnected or under-prioritized.

4

Recommend the next useful move

Translate the findings into practical priorities instead of an unnecessarily large list of activities.
