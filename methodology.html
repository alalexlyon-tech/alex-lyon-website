Methodology

Research → insight → action.

A structured intelligence workflow designed to keep marketing recommendations traceable, prioritized and grounded in evidence.

01
Research

Collect relevant book, reader, market and competitive signals.

02
Verify

Separate verified facts from strong inference, uncertainty and unknowns.

03
Analyze

Connect signals to patterns in positioning, audience and market behavior.

04
Diagnose

Identify the strongest problems, opportunities and information gaps.

05
Identify Gaps

Surface what current evidence does not yet explain.

06
Prioritize

Rank opportunities by relevance, evidence, potential and practicality.

07
Recommend

Convert the strongest findings into clear, defensible next actions.

Verified Fact

Observable

Supported by a credible source or direct evidence.

Strong Inference

Reasoned

Supported by relevant signals but not presented as certainty.

Unknown

Unresolved

An information gap that stays open until evidence supports a conclusion.
